🔐 Randis progress certificate reader

Open a Randis progress certificate (the .json file, or the printable .html page). Everything is checked right here in this page: no internet, nothing sent anywhere. Every hash is re-done, and the current level and the week-by-week history are recomputed from the sessions inside.

What this proves: the file hasn't been changed since it was issued (any edit to a score, date, level, name or the evidence fails the check), and the current level and history really follow from the sessions inside. What it doesn't prove: it's self-issued on the holder's own computer with no outside authority, so someone who rewrites the program could make a file that passes; the proof-of-work makes that take effort, not impossible. Scores are practice tasks, not a normed IQ. To be sure, watch the holder do a few sessions live.
