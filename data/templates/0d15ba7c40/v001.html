Lead · State of the club

The shop gets busier: more members, more projects, one bottleneck

By the club desk · March 14 · 3 min read
Willow Creek Makers Club
The main workbench, drawn for this sample
Founded2019
Members42
OpenTue, Thu, Sat
Tools2 printers, 1 lathe
Next eventRepair café

Six new members joined this month, the most since the club opened. The shop is full on Saturdays, and the 3D printers have a waiting list.

What changed this month

  • Members: 42, up from 36. Four of the six new members came from the last repair café.
  • Tools: the second 3D printer arrived and passed its first test print. The waiting list for printing dropped from two weeks to five days.
  • Projects: three finished: a bird-feeder kit for the school, a repaired church clock, and a bike trailer.
  • Money: the bake sale raised $412, taking the laser cutter fund to $1,240 of $3,000.
"Saturdays are full and Tuesdays are half empty. The easiest new space we have is a weekday evening."Desk analysis

Where we're heading

The bottleneck isn't tools or money any more: it's bench time on Saturdays. Moving one class to Tuesday evening would free about eight bench hours a week, at no cost.

A simple model with your own inputs. It isn't a forecast.

Monthly giving ≈ visitors × the share who give × the average gift. Most groups turn a small share of visitors into regular givers, so start low.

What the model says

Moving the visitors slider changes the result far more than moving the gift slider. So the order is: more people through the door first, then an easy way to give, then thank-you levels.

Explainer

The club explained three ways

For a 10-year-old

A big shared garage where people help each other build and fix things, with tools you couldn't buy on your own.

For a new member

A workshop open three days a week. Members book bench time, take classes, and borrow tools after a short safety check.

For a sponsor

A volunteer-run community space that keeps things out of the landfill and teaches practical skills. Every dollar goes to tools and materials.

The idea underneath

Shared tools → shared skills → fewer things thrown away. The same loop works for a library of things, a garden or a kitchen.

Lead story · Club desk

Saturdays are full, Tuesdays are half empty

Bench bookings by day show where the room to grow is.

Analysis · Source: the club's booking sheet (made up for this sample)

Of the 120 bench hours booked last month, 58 were on Saturdays. Tuesdays had 24 and Thursdays 38. Every Saturday had people waiting for a bench by 11 o'clock.

The shop isn't too small. It's busy at one time and quiet at another.The desk's read

Move the beginners' soldering class from Saturday morning to Tuesday evening. That frees about eight Saturday bench hours a week, and new members often work days anyway.
